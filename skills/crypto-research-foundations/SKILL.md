# Crypto research foundations

## Establish the decision
Reuse known user answers. Record the decision, outcome, horizon, chain/venue,
comparison, evidence cutoff and privacy boundary before collecting more data.
Start with [assets/research-brief.json](assets/research-brief.json).

Ask only missing questions that change the work, two or three at a time. Useful choices:
- Decision: compare plans / assess an existing position / understand a mechanism.
- Outcome: realizable net cash / downside or drawdown / protocol adoption or solvency.
- Evidence: public only / user-supplied exports / authenticated provider configured locally.
Explain what each answer changes; recommend only when a reason is available. These
are examples, not a mandatory questionnaire. Research and inference budgets are
separate from hypothetical investment capital. Never request secrets in chat.

## Build the evidence plan
For each requested fact specify: question → evidence required → measurement →
permitted use → consequence if absent. Prioritize evidence that can change the
ranking of alternatives; do not crawl indiscriminately.

Choose the relevant branch:
- Token discovery, age/history filters and cohorts: `$crypto-token-cohorts`.
- Launch funding, marketing, venue lifecycle and exits: `$crypto-launch-research`.
- Turning research into a simulation cast and rules: `$boyun-world-grounding`.
These branches are optional; do not require a simulation for ordinary research.

## Collect and qualify
Read [references/evidence-contract.md](references/evidence-contract.md) when creating
claims, combining providers or importing observations into model parameters.
Use current official documentation for mechanisms; preserve timestamped provider
responses for measurements. Follow the host's browser/tool rules. No paid service,
key use, transaction or announcement is authorized merely by invoking this skill.
Respect rate limits and explicit spend caps; retain failures and coverage gaps.

Keep observed, derived, assumed, model-judged, disputed and unknown values distinct.
Record units, population, period, retrieval time, method and raw artifact/hash.
Compare source definitions before comparing numbers. Preserve material disagreement;
do not average incompatible sources. Public data cannot establish private intent.

## Deliver
Return a compact decision brief: findings that change the decision, alternatives,
counterevidence, limitations and next evidence needed. Include a research-to-decision
map rather than a link dump. Separate source findings from recommendations.
Do not present a selected sample as a market census, wallet counts as people, volume
as deployable capital, model confidence as calibrated probability, or a paper citation
as validation of this particular world. Freeze the cutoff before comparing plans.

Raw cases, user plans and wallet exports stay in the user's private workspace.
Reusable skills/templates contain methods and fictional or blank examples only.

## Research decisions along the path
Identify lifecycle transitions, extreme/combined shocks and recovery before treating
a fixed plan as complete. Ask what evidence could change a user's response, not only
terminal outcome. Obtain the required path resolution, timestamps, depth/quotes and
control rights; a daily endpoint is not an intraday stress history. Use the stress
policy process in `$boyun-world-grounding` when simulation choices are contingent.
Retain unknown event odds and response failures rather than inventing probabilities.
