# Research-writing references

Reviewed 3 November 2026 through gstack browse. The requested DefiLlama research
index returned a Cloudflare challenge. DL Research's public research hub at
https://www.dlnews.com/research/ was accessible; it describes flagship research
produced with DefiLlama and also commissioned work. The sample below is eight
prominently featured reports, not a verified traffic or quality ranking.
Review covered readable web editions, contents, section openings, conclusions,
figure captions and embedded source attribution; not every downloadable PDF page.

## Article-specific observations

1. [State of DeFi 2025](https://www.dlnews.com/research/internal/state-of-defi-2025/):
   navigable contents and an executive thesis precede sector analysis. Revenue,
   concentration, execution and governance are connected rather than presented
   as disconnected indicators. Borrow the cross-section argument; do not copy
   the very long executive-summary repetition.
2. [Curation as an Infrastructure Layer: kpk](https://www.dlnews.com/research/internal/curation-as-an-infrastructure-layer-kpks-design-philosophy/):
   scope and verification routes lead into governance, bounded automation,
   stress-event case studies and limitations. Liquidity charts support an
   operational question: can capital actually leave? Apply that to exit fills
   and funded actor responses, rather than substituting market cap for liquidity.
3. [Risk-Aware DeFi: Credora](https://www.dlnews.com/research/internal/the-year-of-riskaware-defi-credora-ratings-as-the-1t-unlock-primitive/):
   defines the risk quantity before discussing ratings and case studies. Follow
   the definition-first discipline. Do not import its provider probability claims,
   promotional assertions or rating scale into uncalibrated Boyun worlds.
4. [Tokenized Equities](https://www.dlnews.com/research/internal/tokenized-equities-approach-1b-mark-as-institutional-rails-emerge/):
   analytical headings connect concentration, growth, chain allocation and dated
   catalysts. Figures have named sources and the conclusion names monitoring
   variables. The web text contains inconsistent market-size snapshots; reconcile
   dates and figures in our own work instead of treating source style as infallible.
5. [The Katana Flywheel](https://www.dlnews.com/research/internal/report-katana-flywheel-rethinking-incentives-liquidity-for-sustainability/):
   moves from the incentive problem to architectural components and value flows.
   Borrow the explanation of who supplies capital, who earns and what keeps the
   loop operating. Label designed feedback as a mechanism/hypothesis until tested.
6. [Katana: ve(3,3) at Chain Level](https://www.dlnews.com/research/internal/katana-bringing-ve33-to-the-chain-level/):
   decomposes roles, locking, voting, exit costs and automated delegation before
   discussing risks. Use role/action/constraint diagrams and separate implemented
   mechanics from intended roadmap effects; do not inherit optimistic language.
7. [World Crypto Rankings](https://www.dlnews.com/research/internal/bybit-report-the-world-crypto-rankings/):
   explains relative scores and dimensions before grouping adoption archetypes.
   Borrow explicit units, denominators and archetype comparisons; a ranking,
   behavioral label or simulation count does not become an event probability.
8. [State of RWAfi Q1 2026](https://www.dlnews.com/research/internal/the-state-of-rwafi-q1-2026-report/):
   separates asset classes and distinguishes access, depth, redemption and active
   deployment. Chart sequences explain different functions of capital. Segment
   actor flows and separate sources of risk rather than pooling unlike metrics.

## Apply to Boyun

- Build the section argument before rendering. World Creation defines the model;
  each case explains its conditional thesis, then named worlds and comparisons.
- Put a linked contents page first or second; use informative analytical headings,
  short executive findings, numbered figures and a stable source/claim index.
- Every world needs an ID, why it exists, inputs changed/held constant, probability
  status, actor/event progression, financial consequences and a falsification test.
- Prefer charts of attempted versus executed actions, actor-group cash direction,
  participation/concentration, response delays and matched cash/inventory outcomes.
  A price or volume chart belongs only where it explains a decision mechanism.
- Write the finding before the figure, interpret it immediately after, and state
  an alternative explanation and decision implication. Cite the exact data window.
- Separate full offline reference, artificial stress and partial native records.
  Keep incomplete horizons blank; do not make parameter variants into population
  probabilities. Long-form confidence must never outrun evidence.
- Borrow structure and analytical discipline, not prose, source charts, visual
  branding or unsupported causal/marketing claims. Commissioned research is an
  editorial reference, not independent validation of our simulation.
